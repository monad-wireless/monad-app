Monad Scan is a research instrument for a doctoral study at the Faculty of Informatics and Information Technologies, Slovak University of Technology in Bratislava (FIIT STU). The study estimates how many people are in a room from Wi-Fi and Bluetooth signals.

The beta is invitation only.

Getting started:
- Register in the app with the email address your invitation was sent to. That account is active at once.
- An account with any other address waits until the researcher approves it.
- Complete the short onboarding.

What you do as a tester:
- Take part in short tasks ("quests") inside the FIIT STU building. A quest asks you to walk to a place, scan a printed QR marker with the camera, and sometimes count the people you can see.
- While a quest runs, keep the app open. The phone broadcasts a Bluetooth identity frame so that the study's sensors can detect it.

What the app does not do:
- It does not save photos, video or audio.
- It does not read your location.

What the app sends:
- Quest results, QR scans and Bluetooth records from quests you take part in.
- Crash reports, through Firebase Crashlytics.

Your account:
- It holds your email address and an optional name.
- Change your password in My Account.
- Delete your account in My Account.
- If you forget your password, tap "Forgot Password?" on the sign-in screen and write to the address it shows. The researcher sets a temporary password. Sign in with it, then change it in My Account.

Quests are available only on site at FIIT STU. Away from the building, the quest list is empty.

What to test:
- Registration, sign-in, onboarding and password change.
- Quests: does each step tell you clearly what to do next?
- Anything that crashes, freezes or confuses you.
